# AI Engineering Roadmap
To become an AI Engineer, you must master the following domains:
1. Programming: Python is mandatory.
2. Mathematics: Linear Algebra, Calculus, and Statistics.
3. Machine Learning: Algorithms, Scikit-learn, and model evaluation.
4. Deep Learning: Neural Networks, TensorFlow, or PyTorch.
5. DevOps/MLOps: Docker, Kubernetes, and model deployment (Git).

Best practices: Always version control your datasets and keep track of your model experiments.
